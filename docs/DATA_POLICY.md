# Editorial content, data provenance and safety policy

172 distinct recipes and 158 ingredient names have been authored and stored for offline discovery. Category coverage: Iranian 81, lighter/vegetable dishes 79, snacks 62, home fast food 48 (overlap permitted). **All 172 are still `draft`, not independently approved**. Exactly 16 have individual website pointers; remaining 156 are author-created editorial drafts without individual URLs.

Do NOT copy external recipe text, illustrations or brand assets without permission. Source links are used for factual comparison and traceability, not proof of a commercial content license. Do not promote any recipe to `reviewed` without logged source check, independent quantity/step check, actual food preparation and food-safety review, allergen checks and content/media license clearance. See `docs/CONTENT_RESEARCH_2026-10-20.md` and the structured checklist `content/editorial-review-queue.csv`.

Before production, audit every recipe against actual food prep and measurement realities; confirm grams-versus-pieces, yield, time, safety guidance (USDA FSIS for cooked meats), ingredient identity, potential hidden allergens and storage conditions. Nutritional facts, 'low calorie', 'safe for allergy' and disease-specific diet claims must not be asserted from incomplete draft data.

The `npm run audit:content` gate is intentionally failing until culinary approvals have been received and accurately recorded. No automatic mass approval is permitted.
